Saints and Memorials this week: Monday – St. Laurence, Deacon at Rome martyred in the year 258; Tuesday – St.
Clare of Assisi, companion of St Francis, the first woman to write a religious rule for women, 1253; also on Tuesday
the CofE celebrates St. John Henry Newman, Priest, Oxford Movement leader, theologian and Cardinal, 1890 (the
Roman Church remembers him on 9 October); Thursday - Jeremy Taylor, Anglican Bishop and theologian; Friday –
St. Maximillian Kolbe, Polish Franciscan priest and missionary, who was sent to Auschwitz by the Nazis and who
gave his life there in exchange for another prisoner. He died on 14th August 1941. The prisoner survived the war and
was present in Rome for the canonisation of St. Maximilian in 1982. Saturday 15th August is VJ Day which marks
the surrender of Japan to the Western allies. We pray for world peace.
