                                   Poppy Display for Remembrance-tide

  The display of knitted poppies both inside and outside the church has now been installed – a wonderful
  display of nearly 1900 poppies, with related books and information – all inspired and organised by Sue
  Hemings and with contributions of knitted poppies from a great many from our congregation and wider
                                                  friends.

The church will be open on Saturday and Sunday for 3 weeks, 31st Oct/1st Nov, 7th/8th Nov and 14th/15th Nov
  11.30 – 4.00 on Saturdays and 12.00 – 4.00 on Sundays. Refreshments will be available and all monies
         raised will go to the Royal British Legion. Please do visit and support this worthy cause.

              A huge ‘Thank You’ to all who have contributed in any way to the poppy display.

All Souls. The annual Requiem Mass for All Souls will be on Thursday 5th November at 10.30am. There is
a list at the Back of Church for you to add names of those you wish to be remembered at the Mass.

Mass of Our Lady of Walsingham is next Saturday in church at 10.30am.
Remembrance Sunday will be celebrated next Sunday at 10.30am, with a sung Requiem Mass and The
Two Minutes Silence.

A very big ‘Thank You’ to everyone who was involved in the Dedication Festival and Confirmation last
Sunday – it was a wonderful celebration event, a real highlight in this 150th anniversary year.

Saturday 21st November: Blackthorn Trust Christmas Fair, St Andrew’s Road (just off Tonbridge Road)
10am – 2pm.

Sunday 22nd November: Evensong and Benediction at St Peter’s Folkestone 4pm – all welcome. In
celebration of the Feast of Christ the King. If you would like to go please say, I’m sure lifts can be arranged.

FOR OUR PRAYERS:

We give thanks and glory to God for all the Saints, and we celebrate our participation in the
Communion of Saints. In the Richborough Cycle of Prayer we pray this week for Society Parishes in the
diocese of Canterbury and Chelmsford, for their Priests and people. We pray for all Richborough parishes in
vacancy, especially St Andrew’s Deal and Holy Trinity Ramsgate, for their Churchwardens, and the priests
who are ministering to them. We pray for peace across the world in these turbulent times, and we pray that
God’s Holy Spirit will work in the hearts and minds of the leaders of the nations to promote peace, justice
and the flourishing of all people.
