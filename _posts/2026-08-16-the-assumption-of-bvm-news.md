Do please join us after Mass for Tea, Coffee and Cake in the Vestry Hall - just ask if you don’t know the way!

Services this week: Said Mass on Thursday at 10.30am; next Sunday Sung Mass at 10.30am – both followed by Tea,
Coffee and Cake.

The Summer Organ Recital and Afternoon Tea last Saturday raised over £600 for the organ fund, and was hugely
enjoyed by all who came. A very big Thank You to all who worked so hard to provide the Tea, Cakes and other
refreshments.

Saturday 29th August – FiF Pilgrimage Day at Aylesford Priory, 11am Mass with +Luke, Rosary pm. This was hugely
enjoyed by those who attended last year. Bring a picnic, and a picnic chair! All welcome. I’m sure we can arrange lifts
for any who would like to go but don’t have access to a car. It’s a very short drive away.

Flower Festival and Heritage Weekend – this is a major part of our 150th Anniversary Celebrations and takes place
11th-13th September. Please plan to be here!

Ride & Stride in aid of Friends of Kent Churches also takes place on Saturday 12th September. Catherine Garland
is our parish organiser, and is also looking for sponsorship as she cycles and walks around local churches – Please
support Catherine and sign her sponsorship form at the Back of Church. Money raised is split 50:50 between our
church and the Friends of Kent Churches. The Friends offer grants towards maintenance and repair of Kent churches,
and the PCC will be approaching them for a grant towards our new heating system – so it will be good if once again
we are seen to be supporting the Friends on Ride & Stride day.

The Maidstone Eco-Hub is open until 22nd August at Creative Maidstone, Gabriel's Hill, ME15 6JR. Opening
hours 10am-4pm, Wed to Sat. Practical advice, giveaways and hands-on activities to help you save money, support
nature and make small changes that benefit the environment. The UK is one of the most wildlife-depleted countries in
the world, and recent weather underlines the importance of living in an environmentally sustainable way. See what
you might do to help.
