                 Do please join us after Mass for Tea, Coffee and Cake in the Vestry Hall !!

Thank you to all who supported the concert by the Medway Singers and pianist Keelan Carew. It was
hugely enjoyable and entertaining, a real celebration of the joy of music. Ticket sales and refreshments
brought in £459 which, after deducting costs and Keelan’s fee, gives a contribution of £189 to church funds.

Saturday 17th October:
October Devotions with Bishop Luke, Canterbury Cathedral – Mass at 12 noon, followed by
Devotions and Benediction at 2.30pm. See the poster in the church porch. All welcome !! Do try and
support this event, held specially in our diocese for Catholic Parishes of The Society.

Also Saturday 17th October: Concert by Kent Chamber Choir “Old Favourites and New Classics”,
directed by Daniel Gethin. In church at 7pm. Tickets £15 on the door.

Available at the back of church is a new Booklet “A Foundation of Faith”, written by Eleanor Rele,
edited by Mike Trout and beautifully published by Jonathan Garland. It is a terrific read that brings us close
to the lives and faith of those who brought St Michael’s into being. Excellent value at £2 - full colour
booklet.
